What is Flyoff? #

This project is a new version of a note-taking app called Basalt, built primarily to be a fast and flexible app for notes, checklists, and more.

Flyoff was created by people who needed a place to store notes, write books, and manage checklists, and it has been shaped around the way we use it. We then decided to open the app to others and turn it into something real.

What Flyoff offers? #

Markdown Support

Write in plain Markdown so your notes stay portable and easy to edit anywhere.

No Telemetry

No analytics, no background tracking, and no product data being collected.

Machine Only

Your notes live on your device first, in regular files and folders you control.

LAN Projects

Use shared local-network folders when a project needs to be available across machines.

Open Source

Follow the code, review changes, and help shape the app as it grows.

And a Lot more

Built for daily notes, books, checklists, and the small workflows around them.
